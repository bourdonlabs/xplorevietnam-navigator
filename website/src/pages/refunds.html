Refund Policy

Last updated 30 September 2026

Government fees

Government and official fees are paid to the Vietnamese authorities on your behalf and cannot be refunded by us once paid.

Teach in Vietnam Package

We guarantee you a teaching job once you complete your TESOL, get your degree legalised, and file your visa, work permit and TRC in the right order and on time. If we cannot place you in Vietnam for any reason, we will relocate you for free to a teaching position in Thailand, Cambodia, South Korea, Japan or Costa Rica.

How to ask for a refund

Email info@xplorevietnam.org with your name and the email address you used to pay. Refunds are returned to the card you paid with.
