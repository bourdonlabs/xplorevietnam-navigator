Relocation Services for Teachers

Take your teaching career to Vietnam

Get certified, get placed and get set up, with your visa, work permit and residence card filed in the right order.

Make your move simple with XploreVietnam.

Vietnam is one of Southeast Asia’s fastest-growing education markets, and one of the best places to teach English if you want to live well and still save. Salaries are competitive and the cost of living is low.

The paperwork is the hard part. Your degree and criminal record check need legalising, and your work permit, visa and residence card have to be filed in the right order. We handle it all, from your TESOL training and job placement to your first week in the classroom.

Explore the Program

Teach English in Vietnam.

Your training, job placement, visa and move, planned as one program.

Salary and savings potential

Competitive wages and a low cost of living mean you can enjoy a high quality of life in Vietnam and still build real savings.

Monthly salary

Earn $1,400–$1,600 USD a month (approx. 32–42 million VND), depending on your location, school type and experience.

Low cost of living

Good food, transport and entertainment cost a fraction of what they do at home.

Housing flexibility

Most schools pay an all-in salary rather than providing an apartment. Modern, affordable housing lets you choose your own place for as little as $200–$500 a month.

Savings potential

Because daily expenses are so low, teachers often save $300–$800 a month, enough to fund regional travel, pay down debt or build an investment fund.

120-hour in-class TESOL

You earn your certificate in person in Thailand, then move to Vietnam for your guaranteed placement. Your visa and document legalisation are handled while you study.

Accredited qualification

Earn the TQUK Level 5 Certificate in TESOL, an Ofqual-regulated qualification recognised by schools around the world.

Hands-on teaching practicum

16 hours of real classroom teaching at a local school, so you are confident in front of a class from day one.

Teaching resources

Graduate with a TESOL reference guide and a digital library of lesson plans and activities.

Two-country experience

Train and live in Thailand, then start your career in Vietnam, with cultural orientation for both countries.

Cultural excursions

Curated excursions during the course give you real context for life and teaching in Southeast Asia.

Who can apply

To join the program, you need:

  • Bachelor’s degree or higher
  • Native English speaker
  • Passport from the USA, Canada, the UK, Ireland, Australia, New Zealand or South Africa
  • A local criminal background check
  • A 120-hour in-class TESOL certificate
