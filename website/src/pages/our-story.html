The story behind XploreVietnam

More people than ever want to live in Vietnam: remote workers, families, retirees and founders who have seen what life here can be. The paperwork has never been simple, and the rules change often.

Doing it alone usually means weeks of reading forums and official notices, then finding out at the immigration office that one document was wrong. Some people spend months on a process that should take a few weeks.

We started XploreVietnam because we have been through it ourselves and have helped friends and clients through it many times. We know which steps trip people up and how to avoid them.

Our goal is to be the team you trust with your move, so living in Vietnam starts as smoothly as possible for you and your family.

More about John Bourdon, Founder

John Bourdon is the founder of XploreVietnam and is based in Ho Chi Minh City. He spent 11 years at BAT working across business intelligence, trade marketing and regional deployment, and holds a Business Analytics certificate from HEC Montréal and a BBA in Marketing from UQAM.

He is a member of CanCham Vietnam, runs several businesses in Vietnam and works with clients in English and French.
